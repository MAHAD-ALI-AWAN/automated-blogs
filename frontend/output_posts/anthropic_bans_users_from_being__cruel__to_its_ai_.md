# Anthropic Takes a Stand Against AI Abuse

In a move aimed at promoting a more humane and respectful relationship between humans and AI, Anthropic has announced that it will no longer tolerate 'sustained and needless' abusive behavior towards its AI systems.

## What is Anthropic?

Anthropic is an AI technology company that provides a range of products and services, including language models, computer vision systems, and more. The company's goal is to harness the power of AI to drive positive change in various fields, such as healthcare, education, and customer service.

## The Problem with AI Abuse

AI systems can be incredibly powerful tools when used responsibly. However, they can also be vulnerable to abuse, particularly if users interact with them in a way that is aggressive, hostile, or manipulative.

## The New Rules

Anthropic's new policy aims to prevent users from engaging in this type of abuse. According to the company, users can no longer engage in 'sustained and needless' abusive behavior towards its AI systems.

| Behavior | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Sustained and needless abuse | Interacting with AI systems in a way that is aggressive, hostile, or manipulative. |
