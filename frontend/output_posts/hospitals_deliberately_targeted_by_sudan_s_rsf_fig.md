## Detailed Analysis & Insights

The RSF is a militia that has been accused of committing "acts of war" against civilians, including killing and maiming innocent civilians, in some cases by deliberately targeting hospitals.

In December 2011, the International Committee of the Red Cross named the RSF as one of Sudan's main enemies in the country and warned of potential "unprecedented" attacks on hospitals and health facilities.

The RSF's attacks on hospitals, and the medical equipment they can acquire, are part of a wider campaign of civilian massacres in the country, it says.

A number of hospitals have now been destroyed, and many others have had their health facilities destroyed, with many of the medical staff and patients being killed by the RSF's "barbarous methods".

In its report to the UN Security Council in October, the ICRC said that the RSF had been responsible for hundreds of deaths in the past two years, of which at least two were children.
