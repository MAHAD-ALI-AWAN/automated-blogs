"It's hard to see why this would have happened"

Intel's stock price has been hammered in recent days after the PC market slumped as a result of declining sales of its Core processors.

Intel, a $20 billion startup that went public in June, said that it could not keep pace with demand for its latest processors, forcing it to reduce its PC sales, citing poor results in sales of its Core i9 and Core i7 processors.

Intel is now asking a judge to order the company to take back its sales of certain computers and other goods, including its chips and memory, as part of a class action lawsuit filed last week in Manhattan federal court.

In the suit Intel said it had sold more than 6.3 million processors since the start of 2012, mostly those used in notebooks and other consumer computers.
