## Detailed Analysis & Insights

The company's "behavioral world model" aims to provide a way for AI to understand how human beings behave. It can then make predictions based on that understanding.

The company, which has raised $62.5 million so far, sees the technology as a way for AI to learn how to deal with human beings, though the company is also aiming to make it easier for AI to work with humans.

The company's CEO said the company could be a "real game changer" in the world, as it could help people develop better understanding of their own behavior and even make predictions about how to behave.

This isn't the first time AI-powered prediction has gotten attention.

In 2014, the BBC published an article about a company called Genspace that is developing a "behavioral world model" that tries to predict human behavior.
