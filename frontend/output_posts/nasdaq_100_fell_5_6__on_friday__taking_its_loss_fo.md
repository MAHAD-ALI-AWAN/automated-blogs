## Detailed Analysis & Insights

The Nasdaq Index was down 5.6% on Friday, taking its loss for the week to 10.4% over the last three months, outpacing the S&P 500 by 2.1%.

The Nasdaq, which is trading through the weekend, is now down more than 3.5% this year, its worst run since 2008. Its drop is due in part to worries about the growing use of AI in consumer products and services, which is expected to drive the stock price.

But for far more than one reason, the Nasdaq is doing better despite the gloom from the tech sector. It has rallied sharply over the last few months. In January, the Nasdaq reached a record high of 4,974.08, a level that is still higher than the level of the Dow Jones Industrial Average, which reached its peak of 7,092.43 on Wednesday.
