A Saudi tanker carrying crude oil spilled onto the seafloor near the UAE's port of Al Maktoum on Saturday evening.

According to the Gulf News website, the tanker was off the coast of Al Maktoum, in the eastern Gulf of Aden, when it struck a reef and tipped over onto the beach. The tanker was carrying 32,100 barrels of crude oil, which was being unloaded for export.

The oil spill, which affected around 1,800 square kilometres, has now been contained and is being treated.

The tanker, owned by Saudi Arabia's State Oil Company (SOCOM), is believed to have been carrying crude oil from the Jeddah port of Al Jumeirah.

The Gulf News website reported that the oil had been dumped in the sea, but was not yet known why the oil was spilled.
