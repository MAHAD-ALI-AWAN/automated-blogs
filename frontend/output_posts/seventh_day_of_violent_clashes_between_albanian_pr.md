## Detailed Analysis & Insights

On August 19th, one hundred and sixty-eight protesters were beaten with sticks and iron bars by police during a demonstration outside the Ministry of Justice.

The following day, the police fired tear gas at the crowd and arrested over twenty people.

On August 20th, the protesters were beaten again with sticks and metal bars. The police held them for two hours, without arresting anyone.

On August 21st, a crowd of hundreds of people marched to the Ministry of Justice, and threw stones and Molotov cocktails at the building's windows.

On August 22nd, the demonstrators attempted to push through the barricades and enter the Ministry of Justice. When the police blocked the way, hundreds of protesters threw stones at the police and riot police. Eight policemen were injured in the clashes. When the police tried to disperse the crowd, one of the protestors took out a knife and attacked three of the officers.
