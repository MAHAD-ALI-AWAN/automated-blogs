## Executive Overview

The rapid evolution of modern events continues to reshape industry standards. Recently, **Synology NAS Devices Targeted in Ransomware Attacks Exploiting Patched DSM Flaw** has emerged as a crucial topic of discussion globally.

Synology is urging customers to update the DiskStation Manager (DSM) software powering its Network Attached Storage (NAS) devices following ransomware attacks. The Taiwanese vendor said it observed threat actors exploiting a vulnerability patched in December 2024 to target devices running outdated versions of DSM. "These attacks aim to encrypt data and extort ransom," it said in a statement

## Detailed Analysis & Insights

"The original intent of the patch was to patch the firmware version that was currently installed on the device. However, when we pushed out the patch to the original owner, the patch only applied to the device that he had installed. As a result of this, the user was not aware of the vulnerability until he attempted to install the firmware update. As a result, there is a chance that he could have been affected by the patch."
