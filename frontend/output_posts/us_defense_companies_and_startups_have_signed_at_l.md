**Building a Secure Drone Tech Supply Chain: US Defense Companies Partner with Taiwanese Hardware Startups**

In recent months, the United States defense industry has made significant strides in establishing a secure drone technology supply chain. At least 13 deals have been signed between US defense companies and startups from Taiwan since September 2025, marking a crucial step towards assembling uncrewed boats and aircraft using a "non-red" supply chain.

**Executive Overview & Background**

The recent surge in partnerships between US defense companies and Taiwanese hardware startups is a response to the growing need for secure and reliable drone technology. The US government has been actively seeking ways to bolster its defenses against emerging threats, including China's increasing military presence in the Asia-Pacific region. Taiwan, with its expertise in advanced electronics and manufacturing capabilities, has emerged as an attractive partner for US defense companies.

The "non-red" supply chain refers to a system that avoids relying on critical components sourced from countries like China, which is subject to US sanctions due to national security concerns. By partnering with Taiwanese hardware startups, US defense companies can ensure the secure sourcing of essential components while maintaining a competitive edge in the global market.

**Technical Breakdown / Core Mechanics**

The technical foundation of these partnerships lies in the development of advanced drone technologies, including autonomous systems, sensors, and communication networks. US defense companies are leveraging Taiwanese hardware startups' expertise in areas such as:

1. **Advanced Materials**: Taiwanese startups have developed innovative materials with enhanced strength-to-weight ratios, enabling the creation of lighter yet more resilient drones.
2. **Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML)**: Partnerships between US defense companies and Taiwanese startups are focusing on integrating AI and ML capabilities into drone systems, enhancing their autonomous capabilities and decision-making processes.
3. **Cybersecurity**: The secure sourcing of critical components is a top priority for these partnerships. Taiwanese hardware startups have developed advanced cybersecurity solutions to protect against cyber threats and ensure the integrity of drone systems.

**Market Implications & Industry Impact**
