Privacy Policy

Transparency and safety are core to our platform. Learn how we handle visitors' data, third-party analytics, and advertising compliance.

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Platform Commitment

At Byte Pulse Blogs (accessible from bytepulseblogs.online), one of our primary objectives is safeguarding visitor privacy. Byte Pulse Blogs is an open tech news and publication site. We do not mandate user registration, account creation, or personal data collection to access any of our articles.

2. No Personal Collection

We strictly avoid collecting personal identification details such as names, phone numbers, home addresses, or payment details. Every user can read and explore our content completely anonymously.

3. Log Files & Analytics

We utilize standard web log files to gather non-personal diagnostic data—including IP addresses, browser types, ISPs, timestamps, and referring pages. This data is purely used to monitor performance and optimize website speed.

4. Advertising Partners & Cookies (Google AdSense)

Google is a third-party vendor on our website that uses DART cookies to serve ads to users based on their visits to bytepulseblogs.online and other websites across the internet.

💡 You may choose to decline or customize the use of DART cookies at any time by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy: Google Ad Policies →

5. Third-Party Websites

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to external websites or advertisers linked from our articles. We advise you to review the respective privacy policies of these third-party services for comprehensive details.

6. Children Protection & Consent

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. By continuing to use our platform, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
